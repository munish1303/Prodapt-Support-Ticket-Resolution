Generated by `python scripts/report_dataset_stats.py` from the files actually produced by

## Provenance

* **Source:** `synthetic_template`: complaints and agent resolution notes are composed from 43 hand-authored
  telecom scenarios (`scripts/datagen/scenarios.py`), each one root cause with a known fix, plus generic sentence
  pools (openers, durations, business impact, emotional tone).
* **Labels:** `generator_by_construction`: intent, products and severity come from the scenario (severity is
  bumped one level when a business-impact sentence is included); sentiment is the tone of the emotion
  sentence chosen. No LLM labelling, no human verification.
* **KB articles:** `synthetic_authored`, one per scenario (symptoms, cause, numbered resolution steps).
